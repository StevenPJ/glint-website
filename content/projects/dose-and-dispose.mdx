description: |-
  We are conducting a multicentre, retrospective service evaluation assessing sedation-disposal practices over a two-week period. The primary aim is to quantify the amount of wasted IV sedation agents (midazolam, fentanyl, and buscopan) during endoscopic procedures, with a view to identifying opportunities to reduce waste and improve sustainability.
  This has been presented as an e-Poster at BSG 2026!

  - text: Quantify wasted IV sedation agents across endoscopy units
  - text: Identify opportunities to reduce pharmaceutical waste
  - text: Improve sustainability practices in endoscopy
