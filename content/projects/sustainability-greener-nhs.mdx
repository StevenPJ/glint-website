## Overview

This project assessed the carbon emission impacts of transitioning from traditional face-to-face consultations to virtual gastroenterology clinic appointments during the COVID-19 pandemic, contributing to the NHS's sustainability goals.

## Background

The COVID-19 pandemic accelerated the adoption of virtual consultations across the NHS. This study quantified the environmental benefits of this transition in gastroenterology services.

## Key Findings

- Significant reduction in carbon emissions from patient travel
- Assessment of the environmental impact of digital infrastructure
- Net carbon benefit from virtual consultations

## Status

**Recently submitted for publication**. The manuscript is currently under peer review.

## Impact

This research supports the NHS's commitment to becoming the world's first net-zero national health service by providing evidence for sustainable service delivery models.
