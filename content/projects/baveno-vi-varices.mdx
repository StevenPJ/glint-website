## Overview

This multi-centre audit evaluated the utilization of transient elastography (FibroScan) and adherence to Baveno VI criteria for varices screening across 12 London hospitals, reviewing 828 endoscopy records.

## Publication

**Published in Future Healthcare Journal (2022)**

[Read the full paper](https://www.rcpjournals.org/content/futurehosp/early/2022/01/18/fhj.2021-0130)

## Study Details

- **Hospitals**: 12 London centres
- **Endoscopies Reviewed**: 828
- **Focus**: Transient elastography utilization and Baveno VI guideline adherence

## Presentation

Presented at **BASL (British Association for the Study of the Liver) Virtual Meeting, September 2020**

## Key Findings

- Assessment of current practice against Baveno VI guidelines
- Identification of opportunities to reduce unnecessary endoscopies
- Recommendations for improved patient stratification using non-invasive tests

## Impact

This work supports the safe reduction of surveillance endoscopies in low-risk patients, improving resource utilization and patient experience.
