## Overview

This multi-centre study across 7 London sites examined the standardization of ulcerative colitis (UC) endoscopy reporting, analysing 899 data points to identify variations and opportunities for improvement.

## Publication

**Published in GastroHep (2020)**

[Read the full paper](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/ygh2.427)

## Study Details

- **Sites**: 7 London gastroenterology units
- **Data Points**: 899 endoscopy reports analysed
- **Focus**: Standardization of UC endoscopy reporting

## Presentation

Presented at **ECCO (European Crohn's and Colitis Organisation), February 2020**

## Key Outcomes

- Identified significant variation in UC endoscopy reporting
- Developed recommendations for standardized reporting proforma
- Contributed to improved consistency in UC assessment across London
