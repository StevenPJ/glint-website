## Overview

This multi-centre study examined the quality of care received by adult patients with Inflammatory Bowel Disease (IBD) during the transfer process between healthcare providers across 16 London hospitals.

## Publication

**Published in Frontline Gastroenterology (2020)**

## Key Findings

* Identified gaps in the IBD transfer process
* Recommendations for improving continuity of care
* Best practice guidelines for IBD patient transfers

## Presentation

Poster presentation at **BSG Conference, Glasgow, June 2019**

## Impact

This work has contributed to improved protocols for IBD patient transfers across London gastroenterology services.
