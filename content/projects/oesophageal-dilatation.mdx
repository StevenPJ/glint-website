## Overview

This multi-centre investigation examines the effectiveness of endoscopic dilatation, adherence to clinical guidelines, and risk factors for treatment failure in patients with benign oesophageal strictures.

## Study Details

- **Study Type**: Multi-centre retrospective audit
- **Data Collection**: Commenced January 2024
- **Status**: Sites still being recruited

## Collaboration

This project is being conducted in partnership with the **YaHGuT Research Group** (Yorkshire And Humber Gastroenterology audit/research Trainee network), expanding our collaborative network beyond London.
