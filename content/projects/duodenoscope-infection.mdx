## Overview

This national retrospective multi-centre audit investigates the incidence of infections following ERCP (Endoscopic Retrograde Cholangiopancreatography) procedures performed with reusable duodenoscopes.

## Background

Duodenoscope-associated infections have been a growing concern in endoscopy units worldwide. This study aims to quantify infection rates across multiple UK centres and identify potential risk factors.

## Study Status

Currently in the **write-up phase**. Data collection has been completed and the team is preparing the manuscript for publication.

## Expected Outcomes

- Comprehensive data on infection rates across participating centres
- Identification of risk factors for post-ERCP infections
- Recommendations for infection prevention strategies
