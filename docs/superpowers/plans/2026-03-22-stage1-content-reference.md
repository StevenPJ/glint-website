## Current Projects Page

### Project 1: Dose & Dispose

- **Full title:** Dose & Dispose: The Hidden Cost of Wasted Sedation in Endoscopy
- **Status badge:** Data Analysis
- **Description:** We are conducting a multicentre, retrospective service evaluation assessing sedation-disposal practices over a two-week period. The primary aim is to quantify the amount of wasted IV sedation agents (midazolam, fentanyl, and buscopan) during endoscopic procedures, with a view to identifying opportunities to reduce waste and improve sustainability.
- **Objectives:**
  - Quantify wasted IV sedation agents across endoscopy units
  - Identify opportunities to reduce pharmaceutical waste
  - Improve sustainability practices in endoscopy
- **Timeline:** 2025 - Ongoing
- **Network:** 10+ London centres
- **CTA:** Get Involved

### Project 2: BOS Study

- **Full title:** BOS - Endoscopic Dilation of Benign Oesophageal Strictures
- **Status badge:** Write-Up Phase
- **Description:** A large multicentre evaluation of real-world practice in endoscopic dilation of benign oesophageal strictures. This project has been presented at the MESSE conference in Berlin in October 2025 and involves 11+ centres across the UK.
- **Objectives:**
  - Evaluate real-world dilation practices across UK centres
  - Assess clinical outcomes and safety profiles
  - Identify best practices and standardisation opportunities
- **Timeline:** 2024 - Ongoing
- **Milestone:** Presented at MESSE Conference, Berlin, October 2025
- **Network:** 11+ centres across the UK

## About Us Page

### Hero
- **Title:** About Us
- **Subtitle:** Meet the team behind GLINT - gastroenterology trainees and IMTs dedicated to advancing research and education across London.

### Our Mission
GLINT was founded to create a collaborative network of gastroenterology trainees and IMTs across London, united by a commitment to high-quality research and education. We believe that trainee-led initiatives can make a meaningful contribution to patient care while providing valuable experience for the next generation of gastroenterologists.

### Our Values

1. **Collaboration** — Working together across centres to achieve what no single institution could alone.
2. **Excellence** — Maintaining the highest standards in research design, conduct, and dissemination.
3. **Development** — Supporting trainees to build the skills they need for successful academic careers.
